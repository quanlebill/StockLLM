# Stock Prediction Variables — 1 Year Horizon

Fundamentals and macro dominate at this horizon. Technical signals are weak predictors; valuation, growth, and structural trends matter most.

## Valuation Metrics
- Forward P/E vs. 5-year historical average and sector
- Price-to-Sales (P/S) ratio
- EV/EBITDA
- Price-to-Book (P/B)
- Dividend yield and payout ratio
- Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) implied upside/downside

## Fundamental — Growth & Quality
- Revenue growth rate (YoY, 3-year CAGR)
- EPS growth rate (YoY, 3-year CAGR)
- Net income margin trend
- Return on Equity (ROE) and Return on Invested Capital (ROIC)
- Debt-to-equity ratio and interest coverage
- Free cash flow margin and FCF growth
- Cash conversion cycle
- R&D spend as % of revenue (for tech/pharma)
- Total Addressable Market (TAM) size and penetration

## Competitive & Business Position
- Market share trend
- Competitive moat (pricing power, switching costs, network effects)
- Customer concentration risk
- Product pipeline (for pharma/tech)
- Regulatory risk exposure
- ESG score trend (institutional preference)

## Management & Corporate Actions
- Management track record (EPS beat rate, guidance accuracy)
- Share buyback activity (net share count change)
- M&A activity / acquisition pipeline
- Executive turnover rate
- Insider ownership % and change

## Macro & Sector Structural Trends
- GDP growth forecast
- Federal Funds rate trajectory (Fed dot plot)
- Inflation expectations (10-year breakeven)
- Yield curve shape (2s10s spread)
- Credit cycle stage
- Industry tailwinds / headwinds (regulation, technology disruption)
- Demographic and consumer trend alignment
- Global supply chain exposure

## Analyst & Institutional Signals
- Consensus 12-month price target and revision direction
- Number of buy / hold / sell ratings
- Institutional ownership % and 1-year change
- Hedge fund positioning (13F aggregate)
- Short interest % of float trend

## Sentiment & Alternative Data
- Patent filing activity
- Job posting trends (proxy for growth/contraction)
- Web traffic / app download trends
- Earnings call language (NLP: uncertainty, confidence scores)
- Geopolitical risk exposure (revenue by country)
