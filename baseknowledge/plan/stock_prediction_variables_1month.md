# Stock Prediction Variables — 1 Month Horizon

Mix of technical trends, fundamental catalysts, and macro conditions. Momentum persists but mean-reversion also matters.

## Technical Indicators
- 20/50-day moving average crossover & slope
- Price relative to 52-week high/low
- Trend strength (ADX)
- Volume trend (20-day avg vs. prior month)
- Relative Strength vs. sector / index (RS rating)
- Fibonacci retracement levels
- Monthly pivot points

## Fundamental — Near-Term
- Upcoming earnings date & consensus EPS estimate revision trend
- Revenue growth (TTM and QoQ)
- Gross / operating margin trend
- Free cash flow yield
- Forward P/E vs. sector median
- PEG ratio
- Earnings revision ratio (# upgrades vs. downgrades in past 30 days)

## News & Sentiment
- 30-day aggregate news sentiment
- Analyst consensus rating change (1 month window)
- Short interest change (monthly delta)
- Institutional ownership change (13F filings)
- Management guidance (raised / maintained / lowered)
- Product launch or partnership announcements

## Macro / Sector
- Sector rotation signals (money flow into/out of sector)
- Interest rate trend (10-year yield direction)
- USD strength (DXY) — relevant for multinationals
- Commodity prices (oil, gold, copper) where sector-relevant
- Credit spreads (HY vs. IG)

## Market Sentiment
- AAII Bull/Bear survey
- CNN Fear & Greed Index trend
- Insider trading ratio (buys vs. sells, 30-day)
- Fund flow data (ETF inflows/outflows for sector)

## Calendar Events (within the month)
- FOMC meeting dates
- Major economic releases (GDP, PCE, CPI)
- Sector-specific conferences or trade shows
