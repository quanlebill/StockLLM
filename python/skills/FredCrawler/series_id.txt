--- series_id | series_name | what is this for ---

=== GDP / OUTPUT ===
GDPC1           | Real Gross Domestic Product                                        | Inflation-adjusted GDP level; primary measure of economic output and business cycle tracking
A191RL1Q225SBEA | Real Gross Domestic Product (% Change)                             | Quarter-over-quarter real GDP growth rate; most cited measure of economic expansion/contraction
GDPPOT          | Real Potential Gross Domestic Product                              | CBO estimate of maximum sustainable output; used to compute the output gap (recession/overheating signal)

=== INFLATION ===
CPIAUCSL        | Consumer Price Index: All Urban Consumers (All Items)              | Headline CPI; broadest price measure for consumers; used in cost-of-living adjustments
CPILFESL        | CPI: All Items Less Food and Energy (Core CPI)                     | Core CPI; strips volatile food/energy; Fed and analysts use this to gauge underlying inflation
PCEPI           | Personal Consumption Expenditures Price Index                      | Headline PCE; Fed's primary inflation gauge; broader than CPI, adjusts for substitution
PCEPILFE        | PCE Excluding Food and Energy (Core PCE)                           | Core PCE; Fed's preferred 2% inflation target benchmark for monetary policy decisions
PCETRIM12M159SFRBDAL | Trimmed Mean PCE Inflation Rate (12-month)                  | Dallas Fed trimmed mean; removes extreme price movers; best real-time signal of underlying inflation
MEDCPIM159SFRBCLE   | Median Consumer Price Index                                   | Cleveland Fed median CPI; middle component of CPI distribution; robust to outliers
PPIACO          | Producer Price Index: All Commodities                             | PPI; upstream pricing pressure from producers; leading indicator for future consumer inflation

=== INTEREST RATES ===
FEDFUNDS        | Federal Funds Effective Rate (Monthly)                             | Monthly average overnight interbank lending rate; the Fed's primary policy instrument
DFF             | Federal Funds Effective Rate (Daily)                               | Daily effective fed funds rate; real-time read on monetary policy transmission
DFEDTARL        | Federal Funds Target Range - Lower Limit                           | FOMC lower bound of target rate corridor; signals policy stance
DFEDTARU        | Federal Funds Target Range - Upper Limit                           | FOMC upper bound of target rate corridor; defines the policy rate band
DGS1MO          | Treasury Yield: 1-Month Constant Maturity                          | Shortest-dated Treasury yield; proxy for the risk-free overnight rate expectations
DGS3MO          | Treasury Yield: 3-Month Constant Maturity                          | 3M T-Bill yield; used in yield curve spread (T10Y3M) and as short-rate benchmark
DGS6MO          | Treasury Yield: 6-Month Constant Maturity                          | 6M Treasury yield; part of the short end of the yield curve
DGS1            | Treasury Yield: 1-Year Constant Maturity                           | 1Y Treasury yield; sensitive to near-term rate expectations
DGS2            | Treasury Yield: 2-Year Constant Maturity                           | 2Y Treasury yield; best market-implied signal of near-term Fed policy path
DGS5            | Treasury Yield: 5-Year Constant Maturity                           | 5Y Treasury yield; medium-term growth and inflation expectations
DGS10           | Treasury Yield: 10-Year Constant Maturity                          | 10Y Treasury yield; global benchmark for long-term rates, mortgage pricing, and equity discounting
DGS30           | Treasury Yield: 30-Year Constant Maturity                          | 30Y Treasury yield; long-run inflation + term premium; used in pension/insurance pricing
T10Y2Y          | 10-Year Minus 2-Year Treasury Yield Spread                         | Classic yield curve inversion indicator; negative values historically precede recessions
T10Y3M          | 10-Year Minus 3-Month Treasury Yield Spread                        | NY Fed's preferred recession predictor spread; strong inversion signal before downturns
SOFR            | Secured Overnight Financing Rate                                   | Replaced USD LIBOR; benchmark for dollar derivatives, floating-rate loans, and repo markets
PRIME           | Bank Prime Loan Rate                                               | Base rate for consumer/commercial lending; typically Fed Funds + 300bps
MORTGAGE30US    | 30-Year Fixed Rate Mortgage Average                                | Transmission of Fed policy to the housing market; key driver of housing affordability

=== EMPLOYMENT / LABOR MARKET ===
UNRATE          | Unemployment Rate (U-3, Headline)                                  | Official unemployment rate; share of labor force actively seeking work; primary labor health gauge
U6RATE          | U-6 Total Unemployed + Marginally Attached + Part-time             | Broadest unemployment measure; captures full labor market slack including discouraged workers
U1RATE          | Persons Unemployed 15 Weeks or Longer (U-1)                        | Long-term unemployment rate; signals structural unemployment and difficulty of re-employment
PAYEMS          | All Employees: Total Nonfarm Payrolls                               | Monthly job creation (most-watched jobs report number); primary indicator of labor demand
CIVPART         | Labor Force Participation Rate                                      | Share of working-age population in the labor force; measures supply side of labor market
JTSJOL          | Job Openings: Total Nonfarm (JOLTS)                                 | Number of unfilled job positions; measures labor demand imbalance and tightness

=== MONEY SUPPLY ===
WM2NS           | M2 Money Supply (Weekly)                                           | Broad money supply (cash + deposits + money market); monitored for inflationary pressures
M1SL            | M1 Money Supply (Monthly)                                          | Narrow money supply (cash + checking deposits); liquidity and transaction money in economy

=== TRADE / EXTERNAL SECTOR ===
BOPGSTB         | Trade Balance: Goods and Services (Balance of Payments Basis)      | Net exports of goods and services; component of GDP; reflects competitiveness and capital flows
DTWEXBGS        | Nominal Broad U.S. Dollar Index                                    | Trade-weighted USD strength against broad basket; affects import prices, exports, and corporate earnings

=== CONSUMER & BUSINESS SENTIMENT ===
UMCSENT         | University of Michigan Consumer Sentiment Index                     | Forward-looking consumer confidence; predictor of consumer spending and economic activity
RSAFS           | Advance Retail Sales: Retail Trade and Food Services                | Monthly consumer spending on goods; real-time read on consumer demand (70% of U.S. GDP)

=== HOUSING ===
HOUST           | New Privately-Owned Housing Units Started (Housing Starts)         | Monthly new home construction; leading indicator for construction activity, lumber demand, and growth
EXHOSLUSM495S   | Existing Home Sales                                                | Monthly resale volume; reflects affordability, mortgage rate sensitivity, and housing market health

=== CREDIT & FINANCIAL CONDITIONS ===
BAA10Y          | Moody's Baa Corporate Bond Yield Spread vs 10-Year Treasury        | Investment-grade credit spread; widens during stress; key financial conditions indicator
BAMLH0A0HYM2    | ICE BofA US High Yield Index Option-Adjusted Spread                | Junk bond spread; leading indicator of credit stress, default risk, and risk appetite
VIXCLS          | CBOE Volatility Index (VIX)                                        | Equity market implied volatility; "fear gauge"; spikes during market stress and uncertainty
